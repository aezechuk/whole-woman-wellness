Before you begin

This assessment is a guided reflection, not a test. It's educational and non-clinical, and it doesn't diagnose or measure any health condition.

What we collect: your name and email, your answers to 27 questions, the focus areas you choose, and any reflections you write in your journal.

Who sees what:

  • Your scores and results are visible only to you.
  • Your journal reflections are encrypted and not visible to program staff.
  • Program reports, including to our funder, {{ funder_name }}, share only group summaries. Your name and individual answers are never included.
  • If you choose to share a testimonial, program staff can read it, and it may appear in our funder report, credited the way you choose.
  • Anything you post on the wins wall is visible to everyone in the program, with your first name or anonymously, your choice. You can delete your posts anytime.

Your choices: taking part is voluntary. You can skip the assessment, stop at any time, download your results and reflections, or ask us to delete your data.

How long we keep it: your data is deleted on February 28, 2027, three months after the program ends. You'll get a reminder beforehand to download anything you'd like to keep.

Questions? Contact {{ contact_name }} at {{ contact_email }}.
